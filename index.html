Requirements

From the game's “Estimated Require” panel

In the game, open the Aniimo menu (5 on PC) while in your Homeland, then click the black ! circle next to Ability Distribution to open the Estimated Require panel. Type each number you see there into the box under the matching ability below (same order as the game). The planner then finds Aniimo whose Homeland abilities add up to at least these amounts.

Homeland space

Buildings

Counts start at the most you can place at your RV level; lower any you haven't built (0 means not built). Full-time worker buildings need one Aniimo working in them the whole time. Part-time farm work (Farmland and Woodland plots) only needs help now and then – loosening soil, planting, watering and harvesting each take about 5 seconds per plot – so a few Aniimo can look after many plots. All your Aniimo, including any in the Homebuilding Zone, share your RV level's space limit.

Available Aniimo

The planner can use every Aniimo you can normally catch (base and regional forms), as many of each as it needs. Prismana forms and legendary Aniimo are left out unless you tick them. Untick any Aniimo you don't want suggested.

About the data

The planner uses free fan-made references that are built from the game's own files, plus a few corrections checked in-game by players. After a game update some numbers may be out of date until the data is refreshed.
